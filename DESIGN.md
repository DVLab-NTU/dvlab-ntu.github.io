# DVLab Night Sky

## Overview

The site is one sky drawn two ways. In the dark theme the visitor looks up at a
very deep midnight sky with the Milky Way and a few recognisable constellations
(Big Dipper, Cassiopeia, Orion, Cygnus); controls and cards are liquid glass
floating over it. In the light theme the same stars are printed as a star atlas:
polar coordinate grid, constellation figures, a dashed gold ecliptic and the
Milky Way as a pale band, with vellum glass on top. Gold is the starlight
accent in both and is kept rare.

The sky is generated by `scripts/generate-sky.mjs` (seeded) into
`public/images/sky/night.svg`, `chart.svg` and `src/data/sky-twinkle.json`.

## Colors

Dark is near-black navy, never pure black; light is warm atlas paper with navy ink.

### Primary
- **Starlight Gold** (#f2c75c, text #f4cd6b on dark; print #b8891b, text #7a5706 on paper): active nav item, primary buttons, years on the news timeline, the host tagline, the culture motto. Nowhere else.

### Neutral
- **Midnight** (#010309 → #040915 → #0a1531): sky gradient, top to horizon.
- **Starlight Text** (#e9eef8 / soft #c2cbdf / muted #8e9ab7): dark theme text levels.
- **Atlas Paper** (#f2eee3): light theme ground.
- **Atlas Ink** (#13213f / soft #33405f / muted #59637d): light theme text, chart lines and star dots.

### Named Rules
**The Rare Gold Rule.** Gold marks where you are or what matters most on the screen. Body text, borders and icons stay neutral.

## Typography

**Font:** Jost (variable, self-hosted, a Futura revival — the face family of the Apollo 11 lunar plaque) for Latin; the system Traditional Chinese face for CJK.

**Character:** Geometric and calm; one family, weight and size carry the hierarchy.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (500, clamp(2.25rem → 3.75rem), 1.15): page titles, host name.
- **Headline** (500, clamp(1.375rem → 1.875rem)): section and course titles, member group names.
- **Title** (500, ~1.125rem, 1.4–1.5): card and news titles.
- **Body** (400, ~1.0625rem, 1.7 en / 1.85 zh): reading text, max ~68ch.
- **Label** (500, 0.8125rem, 0.04em): chips, meta, months.

Chinese text gets positive tracking (0.03em body, 0.04em headings); Latin headings get -0.015em.

## Layout

Centered 1120px container with fluid gutters. Reading columns cap at 42–52rem.
The header floats 12px from the top as a rounded glass bar. Home: full-viewport
logo hero with the constellation canvas, a narrow mission column, then a
news timeline (date column, a faint meridian line with a star per entry).
Host: sticky portrait + contacts beside title and bio on desktop; title,
portrait, contacts, bio stacked on mobile. Members: horizontal snap tracks.
Mobile nav opens as a solid sheet under the bar at ≤880px.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth comes from glass, not borders: a translucent fill, a top sheen gradient,
an inset rim (light top edge, faint ring, dark bottom edge) and a soft offset
drop shadow. Hover brightens fill and rim; pressed swaps to an inset shadow.

- **Rim** (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,.17), inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(190,210,255,.075), inset 0 -1px 0 rgba(0,0,0,.32)`)
- **Drop** (`0 10px 30px -10px rgba(0,0,0,.7)`; light `0 8px 22px -10px rgba(19,33,63,.28)`)

## Shapes

Controls and chips are pills. Cards are 16px. The header is 22px. Avatars are circles; the host portrait is a 20px rounded square.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** pill, 40px tall, 0 1.15em padding, 0.9375rem/500.
- **Default (`.btn`, `.btn-ghost`):** glass fill. **Primary (`.btn-primary`):** gold glass. **Icon (`.btn-icon`):** 40px circle.
- **States:** hover brightens; active presses 1px with inset shadow; focus-visible is a 2px ring (gold on dark, ink on paper) offset 3px; disabled 45% opacity.
- External links end with a small arrow-up-right icon.

### Chips
- 28px pill, 0.8125rem/500. `.badge` is gold, `.badge-muted` neutral. Never hover-animated.

### Cards
- 16px radius, panel glass fill, rim + drop shadow, 18–28px padding. Linked cards lift 2px on hover and the title turns gold (underlined in light).

### Inputs
- 44px pill glass field; focus adds a 2px ring.

### Navigation
- Text links as pills on the glass bar; active page uses gold fill and rim. Mobile: icon + label rows in a two-column solid sheet.

### Host contacts
- 46px glass circles with solid Font Awesome marks at one optical size (email, LinkedIn, Facebook, LINE bubble, homepage globe).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** use `.btn` variants for every clickable control so heights and states match.
- **Do** regenerate the sky with `node scripts/generate-sky.mjs` instead of editing the SVGs by hand.
- **Do** keep content legible over the sky: text sits directly on the sky only in reading columns; dense items go in glass cards.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add new accent colors or gradient text; gold is the only accent.
- **Don't** mix filled and outline icon styles within a row.
- **Don't** preload fonts (the page audit rejects it) or add web fonts for Chinese.
- **Don't** use unicode glyphs or emoji as icons.
