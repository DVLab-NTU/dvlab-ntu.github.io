DVLab is directed by Prof. Chung-Yang (Ric) Huang. Research areas include:

- Formal verification
- Quantum circuit synthesis and optimization
- Electronic design automation (EDA)

We welcome Master's students, PhD students, and undergraduate researchers
interested in the areas above, as well as academic and industrial
collaboration. Please contact Prof. Huang at cyhuang@ntu.edu.tw.
